Privacy Policy — WhoCalls Minimal

Effective date: October 1, 2026

WhoCalls Minimal ("the App") is developed and published by CauGiay Hotel ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what data the App and the third-party services it uses handle, how it is used, and who it may be shared with. It applies to the Android application with package name com.whocalls.minimal.coral.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at cghotel1206@gmail.com.

What the App does

WhoCalls Minimal is a caller ID, spam-blocking, and dialer utility. It identifies incoming callers, flags likely spam numbers, lets you block numbers, provides an in-call screen, a dialer, a contacts list, a call history view, a number lookup tool ("Number Tracker"), and "Unseen" — a feature that lets you read the preview text of a notification without marking the underlying message as read.

The App is free to use. It does not require creating an account, does not require signing in, and does not sell any in-app purchases or subscriptions — every feature is available without payment or registration.

Data processed on your device (not sent to us)

To provide its core features, the App requests the following device permissions. The data these permissions expose to the App is processed locally on your device to power the corresponding feature. We do not collect, transmit, or store this data on our own servers, and it is not shared with us:

PermissionWhy the App requests it
Call log (READ_CALL_LOG)To show your call history and attach caller ID / spam-risk labels to numbers you've called or been called by.
Contacts (READ_CONTACTS, WRITE_CONTACTS)To display the names of people in your contacts when they call, and to let you add or edit contacts from within the App.
Phone state and calls (READ_PHONE_STATE, ANSWER_PHONE_CALLS, CALL_PHONE, MANAGE_OWN_CALLS)To detect incoming calls, let you answer or place calls from the dialer, and manage the App's own call sessions when it acts as your phone/dialer app.
Call screening (acts as a call-screening app)To automatically decline or flag calls from numbers you have chosen to block.
In-call service (acts as your default phone app's in-call UI)To show the in-call screen (caller ID, spam risk, call controls) while you are on a call, when you set the App as your default phone app.
Full-screen intent (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT)To show the full-screen incoming-call screen when your phone rings.
Notification access — "Unseen" (Notification Listener)With your explicit permission, granted separately in your device's Settings, this lets the App read the preview text of incoming notifications so you can view a message's content inside the App without marking the original notification/message as read. This is an optional feature: the App cannot read notification content unless you manually enable "Notification access" for it in Android Settings, and you can revoke that access at any time. Notification previews read this way are processed and stored on your device only; they are not uploaded to us or to any third party.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)To show you alerts from the App, such as spam-call warnings or lookup results.

Your contacts, call history, and notification previews never leave your device through any feature we built. They are not transmitted to CauGiay Hotel's servers and are not shared with the advertising, analytics, or attribution services described below.

Data collected by third-party services

Like most free apps, WhoCalls Minimal uses third-party software development kits (SDKs) for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, and attribution. These SDKs are integrated into the App and, independently of the on-device features above, collect certain data from your device and transmit it to their respective providers over an encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connection. We do not control how these third parties process the data once received; each provider's own privacy policy governs that processing.

The services currently integrated in the App are:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) — collects advertising identifiers (Advertising ID), device information, and app-interaction data to support advertising delivery and measurement. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses data.
  • Meta Audience Network (Facebook SDK) — collects advertising identifiers and app-event data (automatic SDK initialization and event logging are enabled) to support advertising. See Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • AppLovin, including its mediation partners Vungle, Mintegral, Unity Ads, and Pangle — collect advertising identifiers and device/app-interaction data to mediate and deliver ads from multiple ad networks. See AppLovin's Privacy Policy.
  • Adjust — collects advertising identifiers and installation/attribution data to measure how the App was installed and used. See Adjust's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase — specifically Firebase Analytics (app usage/interaction events), Firebase Crashlytics (crash logs and diagnostic data to help us fix stability issues), and Firebase Remote Config / Realtime Database (app configuration data). See Google's Privacy Policy.

These SDKs are built into the App and may begin operating (including collecting an advertising/device identifier) as soon as the App is used, independent of whether a given ad is actually displayed to you in the current App version. The data types these SDKs can collect are: advertising/device identifiers, app interaction events, and crash/diagnostic logs. None of these SDKs are given access to your contacts, call log, call content, or notification previews — those remain on-device as described above.

Where your region's law requires a consent step before these SDKs may operate (for example under GDPR or similar frameworks), the App will present that choice before the relevant SDK is allowed to collect data.

Data we ourselves collect

CauGiay Hotel does not operate its own backend servers for this App and does not directly collect, store, or sell personal data outside of what is described above for the third-party SDKs. We do not have access to your contacts, call log, or message previews.

Children's privacy

WhoCalls Minimal is a general-purpose phone utility and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, contact us at cghotel1206@gmail.com and we will take appropriate steps.

Data retention and deletion

  • On-device data (contacts, call log entries, blocked numbers, notification previews read via Unseen) is stored only on your device and is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.
  • Because the App has no account system, there is no server-side account or profile for us to delete.
  • To stop data collection by the third-party SDKs described above, you can revoke the relevant device permission (e.g. Notification access) in Android Settings, reset your device's Advertising ID, or uninstall the App.

Security

We rely on Android's standard app sandboxing for on-device data, and all data sent by the third-party SDKs described above is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we and our SDK providers take reasonable measures to protect data in transit.

Your choices

  • You can deny or revoke any of the permissions listed above at any time in Android Settings; some features (e.g. caller ID, call screening, Unseen) will stop working if the related permission is revoked.
  • You can reset or limit your device's Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy to limit ad personalization.
  • You can uninstall the App at any time, which removes all on-device data associated with it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add a new feature or integrate a new third-party service. We will post the updated policy at this same URL with a new effective date.

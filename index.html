FAQ
Is WhoCalls Minimal free?

Yes. Every feature is free. There is no account, no sign-up, and no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Do I have to create an account?

No. The app opens straight to its features — there is no login or registration.

How does call blocking work?

When you set WhoCalls Minimal as your default phone app, it acts as a call-screening app and automatically declines or silences calls from numbers on your block list.

What is 'Unseen'?

Unseen is an optional feature that lets you read a message notification's preview without marking it as read. It only works after you grant Notification access in your device Settings, and previews stay on your device.

What data does the app collect?

Your contacts, call log and message previews are processed on your device and are not sent to us. Like most free apps, it uses third-party SDKs (ads, analytics, crash reporting) that collect device/advertising identifiers — details are in the Privacy Policy.
